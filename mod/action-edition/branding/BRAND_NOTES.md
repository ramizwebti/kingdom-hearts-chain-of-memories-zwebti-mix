# Zwebti Mix artwork

Classic Chain of Memories logo styling with a gold, Final Mix-inspired **Zwebti Mix** subtitle. The app icon is the original Kingdom Hearts heart, with no words.

- `zwebti-mix-logo.png`: transparent 1525×1031 logo, edited with the built-in image generator from the original GBA logo reference.
- `zwebti-mix-icon.png`: exact upstream `port/assets/icon.png` (512×512 RGBA).
- `zwebti-mix-icon.bmp`: exact upstream `port/assets/icon.bmp` (128×128 RGB).
- `zwebti-mix.ico`: ten Windows sizes, 16–256 pixels, converted from that original heart.

Logo reference: [original Chain of Memories artwork](https://www.khwiki.com/File:Kingdom_Hearts_Chain_of_Memories_Logo_KHCOM.png), whose file history identifies Nintendo of Europe as its source. Heart source: the pinned ReKHCoM project assets. Game marks remain their owners' artwork and are not covered by the code's CC0 license. Zwebti Mix is an independent fan modification.
